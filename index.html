Before you book

The questions we get asked.

What does the price cover?

A standard passenger vehicle, up to seven side and rear windows. Windshields and sunroofs are not included. SUVs and trucks add $50. Exotic, luxury and vintage vehicles are quoted individually.From their site

Which film should I pick?

Metallic is the budget option but can slightly affect phone, GPS and radio signal. Carbon and both ceramics do not. Ceramic rejects the most heat and blocks up to 99% of UV.From their site

How dark can I go?

Darker film is allowed on the rear side and back windows than on the windshield and front side windows. California's rules are specific, so ask the shop what is legal for your vehicle before choosing.General guidance

How do I look after it?

Their blog advises leaving the windows up for three to five days after fitting, using ammonia-free cleaners and avoiding the film edges. Confirm the aftercare for your film with the shop.From their blog

Is there a warranty?

Their site lists a lifetime warranty on metalized window film and any upgrades, under the Tesla offer. Ask the shop what is warranted on the film you choose, and get it in writing.From their site
